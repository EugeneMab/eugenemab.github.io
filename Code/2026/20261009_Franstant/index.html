Franstant (Francais Constant)

1. Introduction

Welcome to Franstant. It is an artificial language built upon French, but without conjugation. The design goal is to simplify grammar while retaining the core vocabulary and structure of French. Verbs do not change, and nouns remain in masculine singular forms.

2. Detailed Examples

Instead of saying "Je vais au restaurant avec mes amis" (I am going to the restaurant with my friends), we say: "moi aller à le restaurant avec mon les ami." We use "mon les" to indicate plural possession. Nouns do not have plural forms with "-s".

Other rules include: no apostrophes (e.g., d'eau becomes de le eau), no conjugations (the infinitive is used for all persons), and questions use the fixed structure 'être ce que'.

3. Sample Passages

The Tower of Babel

Now the whole world had one language and a common speech.

The Lord's Prayer

Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name, your kingdom come, your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven.

4. Schemas and Pronunciation

The letters and pronunciation are exactly the same as in standard French. The alphabet has 26 letters.
